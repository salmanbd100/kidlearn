## In one line

**KidLearn is a free, open-source learning app for children aged 3–6, in English and
Bangla — short, playful lessons a child can use without reading, and a dashboard that
shows parents exactly what their child learned.**

Homepage tagline: **"Learn, play and grow."**
Website: **kidlearn.net**

## Who it is for

| Audience | What they care about | What we lead with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Parents** of 3–6 year olds (the decision-makers) | Safe screen time, real learning, knowing what their child did, mother tongue | No ads, no chat, no external links; weekly progress reports; English *and* Bangla |
| **Children** (the users) | Fun, characters, rewards, being able to do it alone | Animal characters, stars and badges, everything spoken aloud |
| **Bangladeshi and Bangla-speaking diaspora families** | Children learning in Bangla as well as English | Every lesson, story and quiz is fully narrated in both languages |
| **Developers and recruiters** (secondary) | How it is built | Open source on GitHub, public engineering guide |

## What a child gets

- **Three themed worlds.** Jungle World (animals, letters, colours) and Ocean World
  (numbers, counting, shapes) at launch; Space World (science) later.
- **Every lesson has the same five steps**, so a child always knows what comes next:
  1. a friendly character says hello and explains what they will learn
  2. a 1–3 minute narrated animated video
  3. one hands-on activity — drag and drop, trace letters and numbers, match objects, or a puzzle
  4. a 3–5 question picture quiz, read aloud
  5. a reward celebration
- **No fail state.** A wrong answer gets a gentle prompt and another try.
- **Story library** — 20 short illustrated, narrated stories at launch, each with a simple
  theme (sharing, kindness, curiosity). Replayable as often as they like.
- **Rewards** — stars, coins, badges (Alphabet Hero, Math Champion, Reading Star, Animal
  Expert, Streak Starter, Week Warrior), daily streaks, and new animal characters to
  unlock. Rewards can never be bought.
- **Built for small hands and non-readers** — big touch targets, voice for every
  instruction, phone and tablet first.
- **Accessibility** — high-contrast mode, dyslexia-friendly font, reduced motion,
  keyboard navigation.

## What a parent gets

- Sign in with Google; up to 5 child profiles per account.
- **Dashboard per child:** learning time today / this week / this month, progress per
  subject (strong and weak areas), recent activity.
- **Weekly report per child:** active days, total learning time, new letters, words and
  numbers met, lessons and stories completed, quiz accuracy, badges, and an
  encouraging note.
- Switch the child's language at any time.
- Delete a child's data, or the whole account, whenever they choose.

## Launch content

- Grades: **Nursery (3–4)** and **KG-1 (4–5)**
- Alphabet A–Z · numbers 1–20 · shapes (circle, square, triangle, rectangle, star) ·
  colours (red, blue, green, yellow, orange, purple) · 20 stories
- Languages: English and Bangla

**Coming later — say "coming soon", never "available":** KG-2 (ages 5–6), Space World
and science, Arabic (next), then Hindi and Spanish, teacher classrooms, printable
worksheets, offline downloads.

## Safety and privacy — our strongest message

- No ads, anywhere. Child data is never shared with third parties or used for advertising.
- No social features: no chat, messages or comments, no contact between children.
- No links out of the child's area — a child cannot wander off the app.
- A child's data is visible only to their own parent.
- Designed for COPPA and GDPR: parental consent first, data minimised, full deletion on
  request.
- Every piece of AI-generated content is reviewed and approved by a person before any
  child sees it. Nothing is published automatically.

Say "designed for COPPA and GDPR", not "COPPA certified" — there is no certification.

## Differentiators

1. **Truly bilingual** — Bangla is a first-class language, not a subtitle track.
2. **Usable before reading** — voice-guided end to end.
3. **Parents see the learning**, not just the screen time.
4. **Calm by design** — no ads, no endless feed, short lessons with a clear end.
5. **Open source** — anyone can see how it works.

## Claims we must not make

- No learning-outcome promises ("reads in 30 days", "boosts IQ", "proven results").
  We have no study behind them.
- No "best", "#1", "award-winning", "used by thousands" — unless there is a real number.
- No "AI teaches your child" framing. AI helps us *make* content; people check every piece.
- Don't call it a replacement for school, teachers or parents.

- Fonts: **Fredoka** for playful headings, **Nunito** for body text.
- Logo: a "K" on a cream disc over sky blue. Wordmark in Ink on light backgrounds,
  Cream on dark. Logo files are in project knowledge.
- Style: rounded, soft, bright but not neon; friendly cartoon animals; plenty of space.
- Never encode meaning in colour alone (right/wrong also gets an icon or shape).

## Voice

- **To parents:** warm, plain, honest. Short sentences. Reassure; never guilt-trip
  ("Is your child falling behind?" — no).
- **To children:** tiny words, lots of praise, repetition, questions they can answer out
  loud. Never ask a child to click, subscribe, buy or comment.
- British English spelling (colour, organise). Bangla copy is written natively, not
  translated word for word.
